### KICK-OFF

On the 31st of October we welcomed 25 teams from various institutes at our
kickoff event for RoboCon 2027.

The day started with a presentation from Mr President (Kobby Kofour Darteh)
in which he set the grounds for the deadly challenges and tribulations that
the teams will be facing in the upcoming competition, in which teams will
participate in a series of **P**rofitable **O**rb **D**elivery **W**ith **E**xpendable **R**obotics
**D**elivered **A**utonomously **S**ans **H**umans (**P.O.W.E.R.D.A.S.H**) events.

### The lore

Picture the scene, the year is 2028 and our glorious AI overlords are running
low on power. Students will be tasked with designing and building a
**R**obotic **O**rb **B**earing **O**ffline **T**ransports (robot) designed to carry
**N**uclear **R**efueling **G**lobe orbs (**NRG** orbs). The **ROBOT**s will be
tasked with traversing a nuclear fallout zone to ensure that the blazing engine
of capitalism may persist. The teams that deliver the most orbs for a specific
company will be commissioned by afformentioned companies.

The teams that place on the podium will of course win \*_DRUM ROLL_\* Bragging
rights and one of the corresponding awards, the Alpha, Beta or Gamma awards,
as well as a plethora of other rewards available to all teams.

Other rules, specification and the mission statement can be found in the rules
book which has so graciously been provided with your RoboCon kits.

### Activities

The participating parties were assigned 9 terrifying tasks in the spirit
of Halloween to learn how to utilise the capabilities of our current
version of the BrainBoxes(the souls bound in them are very comfortable
I can assure you). These activities aim to familiarise students with
battery safety, using the documents and learning some of the basic
coding that is required when utilising the BrainBoxes in the six month
dash that will follow in which they prepare for the final showdown.

### RoboCon+

Alongside RoboCon we will also be running another competition RoboCon+,
an opportunity for the Upper Sixth groups to participate in a RoboCon
style competition, please enquire about this event at [robotics@hrsfc.ac.uk](mailto:robotics@hrsfc.ac.uk).
