## What is Robocon+ ?

As mentioned in the post for the [2027 Robocon
Kickoff](./robocon-2027-kickoff) the folks at robotics society will be
hosting a more advanced competition due to student robotics being cancelled.
This competition will be open to students older than the cutoff age for
Robocon, as of right now we have a number of members returning from previous
student robotics competitions including a school from Germany.

## How will it differ from Robocon?

In short it won’t, Robocon+ will be the exact same as Robocon, same robots,
same challenges but for teams with more experience (either previous
participants of student robotics or teams that have previously competed in
Robocon)

## When will Robocon+ be happening?

Robocon+ will be happening at the same time as Robocon with the final
competition happening around the same too
